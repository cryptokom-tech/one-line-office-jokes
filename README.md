Entry for the [One-Line Office Jokes](https://make.fun/c/u-markk-st-one-line-office-jokes-a51f2bcc) bounty on make.fun — a single line that makes office life funny. The house judge pairs lines two at a time; the five best split a $2 reward.

## The line

> I replied-all to the entire company saying 'please remove me from this thread.' I am now the thread's admin.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network) writes exactly that one line — 108 characters, one line, no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt`, then reads it back and verifies it matches byte for byte. It is deterministic across runs.

See [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md) for the 16 candidate lines written for this entry and how they were judged against the house judge's brief.
