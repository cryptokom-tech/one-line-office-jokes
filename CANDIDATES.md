# Candidate lines — local judging

Judged against the house judge's brief, criteria in order:
1. A single-line joke about office life.
2. Turns on a real detail of office life (meetings, email, printer, coffee machine).
3. Lands its laugh in the last few words.
4. Clear on one reading, no setup that needs explaining.
5. Fresh wording, not a joke already going round the internet.

## Submitted (PRIMARY)

**B — "I replied-all to the entire company saying 'please remove me from this thread.' I am now the thread's admin."** (108 chars)

- 1: single-line office joke. 2: reply-all, the canonical office detail. 3: the laugh lands in the last four words — asking to be removed ends in becoming the thread's admin, an ironic reversal. 4: one reading, no explanation needed. 5: the turn (removal request → admin) is fresh; the setup itself is already a joke (replying-all to complain about reply-all), so the line carries two turns.
